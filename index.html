How an order works

From the QR code on the table to the bill – in six steps, without a notepad and without running back and forth. Steps 1 and 2 are your digital menu; from step 3 on, the ordering system Smart ServiceHub™ takes over.

At the table – your guest

  1. 1Menu

    Scan the QR code

    Your guest scans the code on the table with the normal phone camera. Every table has its own code, so every order lands at the right table.

  2. 2Menu

    The menu opens

    Instantly, in their own language and with photos – right in the browser, without installing an app.

  3. 3Ordering

    Order from the phone

    A few taps put dishes and drinks in the cart, with a note like “medium” if needed. One tap on “Send order” – done.

In the kitchen, at the bar, in service and at the till

  1. 4Ordering

    Kitchen and bar see it instantly

    The kitchen only sees the food, the bar only the drinks – sorted by table, with every note. If you like, a push notification goes to the phone as well.

  2. 5Ordering

    Service brings it to the table

    The Service Hub shows every table at a glance: green = free, orange = active, red = wants to pay. When a dish is ready, the bell appears – and your team serves it.

  3. 6Ordering

    Get paid

    When the guest taps “Request bill”, the bill is already put together at the till – with your till numbers if you like. You key it into your own till as usual.
