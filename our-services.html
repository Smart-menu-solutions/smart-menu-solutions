Beyond the ordinary

Elevate your business with digital menus. Smart Menu Solutions empowers restaurants, cafés, bars and hotels to deliver a cutting-edge menu experience that's quick, convenient, and perpetually updated.

Services

Whether you're a restaurant, café, food truck, bar, or takeaway service, Smart Menu Solutions provides an affordable and professional digital menu platform designed to help small businesses grow.

Smart Start

Perfect for cafés, food trucks, kiosks, and small businesses getting started with digital menus.

Smart Pro

Ideal for growing restaurants and hospitality businesses that need greater flexibility.

Smart Premium

Designed for busy restaurants, hotel restaurants, chains, and businesses seeking a fully professional solution.

Add-on

Extend any Smart Menu plan with optional extras — from eye-catching dish photos to smart recommendations and weekly visitor insights, add exactly what your business needs, whenever you need it.

Smart DishPhoto™

Add a real photo to every dish on your menu — a polished, appetizing look that helps guests decide faster and order with confidence.

Smart FoodMatch™

The intelligent add-on for every Smart Menu — guests answer 3 quick questions and get an instant personalized recommendation.

Smart WeeklyReport™

A weekly email with visitor stats for your menu — see which categories and dishes guests view most, no login required.

Most popular

Smart ServiceHub™

Guests order via QR code while waiter, kitchen, bar and cashier stay perfectly in sync in real time — less paper, fewer errors, faster service.

Already a customer? You can add any of these add-ons to your plan at any time - you'll only pay the pro-rated amount for the rest of your current plan year, and it renews automatically from then on.
