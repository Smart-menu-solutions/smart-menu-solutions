How to order your digital menu

We've made the process simple, affordable, and completely hassle-free. Get started in minutes.

1

Choose your plan

Select the package that best fits your business: Smart Start, Smart Pro, or Smart Premium.

2

Complete your details

3

Submit your request

5

Display your QR code on tables, counters, menus, flyers, or takeaway packaging and start serving customers instantly.
