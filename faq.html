FAQ

Our journey began with a simple idea: to digitalise restaurants with a modern, eye-catching brand. Here's what people usually ask us before getting started.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We handle the entire setup for you.

How do I get started?

Choose a plan, pay securely online and upload your menu (PDF) and, if booked, your photos right afterwards.

Do customers need an app?

No. They simply scan the QR code with their phone camera.

Do you provide support?

Yes. We're here to help whenever you need assistance.

Can I update my menu later?

Yes. Menu updates are available based on your plan.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time as your business grows.

What happens after I pay?

You go straight to the upload page for your menu. The link is also in your confirmation email if you'd rather upload later. We then review your menu and contact you with the next steps.
